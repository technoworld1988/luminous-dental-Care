Root canal treatment

Caps and bridges

Fixed braces

Meet Dr. Ha-Meem Fattaha

BDS (Dhaka) · Oral and dental surgeon

Dr. Ha-Meem Fattaha is the dental surgeon at Luminous Dental Care. She is also a consultant in the dental unit at Ibn Sina Diagnostic and Consultation Center, Rikabibazar.

At her own chamber in Subidbazar she treats adults and children, from a first checkup to root canals, braces and implants.
